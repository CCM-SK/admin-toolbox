Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:
- harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- insults, threats, intimidation, or deliberately hostile behavior.
- trolling, inflammatory comments, or repeated disruption of discussions.
- publishing someone's private or sensitive information without permission.
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

Enforcement
Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including removing comments or contributions, closing discussions, or restricting participation in the project.
Maintainers will aim to handle situations fairly and respectfully.

Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers rather than escalating the situation publicly.
For security vulnerabilities or accidentally disclosed sensitive information, please follow the instructions in SECURITY.md instead.

Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions related to Admin Toolbox, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project communication.

Attribution
This Code of Conduct is based on common open-source community standards and may be adapted as the project evolves.
